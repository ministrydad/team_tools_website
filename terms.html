  • You hold all licenses, permissions, and rights needed for that use, or
  • The content is in the public domain, or
  • The content is your own original work, or

In practice, most communities meet this through a blanket license. The common ones in the United States are CCLI (Christian Copyright Licensing International), OneLicense, and Christian Copyright Solutions. Which you need depends on the songs you use and how you use them. Note that a basic CCLI church copyright license and a CCLI streaming license cover different things, and that a license held by a member's home church does not automatically extend to your Tres Dias or Emmaus community as a separate organization.

If you are not sure whether your community is covered, find out before your next weekend. This is genuinely worth a phone call to CCLI.
